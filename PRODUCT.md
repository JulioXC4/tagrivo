# Tagrivo

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, React Islands, Astro Content Collections. Static Cloudflare deployment; no backend, database, or authentication.

## Users

Small US sellers and entrepreneurs using Etsy, Shopify, and eBay to ship orders.

## Product Purpose

Help sellers find, compare, and choose thermal shipping-label printers through sourced specifications, compatibility checks, and transparent cost estimates.

## Positioning

Independent editorial advisor. Affiliate revenue cannot determine recommendations. No fabricated testing, testimonials, prices, or compatibility claims.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Five-step Finder; two/three-printer comparison; cost calculator; sourced product details; original buying guides; about, methodology, disclosure, and privacy pages. Public content in US English. Amazon links stay absent until explicitly configured. Prices in tools are labeled user-editable planning assumptions, never live offers.

## Brand Commitments

The user selected the first green Editorial Premium concept and explicitly requested implementation without new proposals. Preserve elegant serif typography, white surfaces, deep pine, generous whitespace, and “Print smarter. Ship better.” The October 29, 2026 image update replaces manufacturer photographs with original, clearly disclosed conceptual illustrations of printing workflows. Never present them as pictures of the actual named models. Preserve the existing functional tools and verified product data.

## Evidence on Hand

User brief and selected generated concept. Manufacturer product pages and support documentation are cited in the product catalog. No hands-on test results, private product photos, affiliate tag, or legal business identity were supplied.

## Product Principles

- Explain the trade-offs and unknowns.
- Filter incompatibilities before ranking.
- Keep factual specifications separate from editorial judgment and planning inputs.
- Keep the reading experience calm and the tools useful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target; keyboard controls, visible focus, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and a dedicated dark theme.
