The QL-1100 and QL-1110NWB share a 300 dpi direct-thermal, DK-roll approach. The buying decision turns largely on connectivity: a USB workstation versus network and Bluetooth interfaces. The QL-1100c and QL-1110NWBc are separate variants and must not inherit support claims automatically.

**Consider QL-1100 for one supported computer. Consider QL-1110NWB for a verified network workflow.** Both need a consumables calculation based on compatible DK stock. This comparison uses manufacturer documentation, not hands-on tests.

## What the specifications mean

| Feature | QL-1100 | QL-1110NWB |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resolution | 300 dpi | 300 dpi |
| Rated maximum speed | 110 mm/s | 110 mm/s |
| Automatic cutter | Yes | Yes |
| USB | Yes | Yes |
| Ethernet / Wi-Fi | No | Yes |
| Bluetooth interface | No | Yes; OS / app dependent |
| Consumables | Compatible Brother DK rolls | Compatible Brother DK rolls |
| Automatic Crop | Windows only | Windows only |

The rated linear speed is not a promise of 69 completed 4 × 6 shipping labels per minute. Brother’s labels-per-minute claims use specified label types and conditions. Do not transfer a standard address-label result to a larger shipping format.

## USB or shared access?

QL-1100 keeps a supported computer in the print path. Confirm the current Brother driver and OS release; the presence of a USB Host interface does not make every phone app compatible. This can be a deliberate trade-off for a fixed desk rather than a missing feature you will later enable in software.

QL-1110NWB adds network interfaces and Bluetooth. For AirPrint, Brother requires a wired or wireless network connection: Bluetooth is not the AirPrint transport. Confirm device discovery, app support and the paper format. A network isolation setting can stop a valid printer from appearing even when internet access works.

Tagrivo’s [Finder](/printer-finder/) uses conservative documented device / connection routes. It does not interpret the presence of Bluetooth as proof of universal iPhone, Android or Mac shipping-PDF printing. Check the linked [Brother support matrix](https://support.brother.com/g/b/oslist.aspx?c=us&lang=en&prod=lpql1110nwbeus) for your exact OS.

## DK rolls are a separate buying decision

Both models support specified DK die-cut and continuous supplies. They are not generic loose-fanfold printers. The automatic cutter is useful for compatible continuous stock, but roll width, supported length, driver settings and adhesive still matter.

For example, the US brochure lists DK-1247 large shipping labels as 4.07 × 6.4 inches. That is not exactly a 4 × 6 inch blank. Match the provider’s document to the selected stock and driver format. Do not force a size merely because the marketing description says “shipping.” Our [media guide](/guides/choose-compatible-thermal-labels/) explains the distinction.

Use the [calculator](/cost-calculator/) with a current hardware quote and the cost per usable DK label. We do not have a live price feed or evidence that either printer is always cheaper to own.
