For the Phomemo PM-241BT, compatibility is a route through software, not just a list of operating-system logos. The manufacturer also writes the name as PM241-BT or PM-241-BT. Do not confuse this 203 dpi model with a PM344-WF Wi-Fi printer or another Phomemo label maker.

**The documented starting routes are Labelife over Bluetooth on a phone and the exact-model USB driver on a computer.** There is no basis here to describe this model as AirPrint or Wi-Fi hardware. We have not physically tested these workflows.

## iPhone and Android: import the PDF into Labelife

Phomemo links Labelife for mobile printing. Start with an original 4 × 6 shipping PDF from the selling platform or postage provider, then use the app’s supported import and printer-selection process. Confirm that the actual label format and all required shipping information survive the preview.

A Bluetooth pairing screen does not prove a print job can travel from any app. Nor does a PDF displayed at the right shape prove its page dimensions are correct. If a marketplace app cannot pass the file to the supported route, investigate its documented browser/download workflow rather than assuming a printer fault.

## Windows and macOS: verify the USB driver

The manufacturer product page links software and drivers. Check the exact PM241-BT driver, OS release, CPU requirements and cable before buying. On a modern laptop, a connector adapter does not certify software compatibility.

Tagrivo conservatively uses USB for desktop matching. Manufacturer material describing a Bluetooth feature somewhere in the product family is not enough to promise an operating-system-wide Bluetooth driver for this model. If you require desktop Bluetooth, obtain exact-model instructions for that OS first.

## Label stock: one unresolved manufacturer discrepancy

The product page supports 4 × 6 shipping labels and states 203 dpi with a rated speed up to 150 mm/s. It also presents conflicting media-width ranges in different sections. We therefore do not publish a single precise minimum/maximum as a settled fact.

For non-4 × 6 work, confirm the current manual and your exact hardware revision before ordering stock. Phomemo also warns against HUOJI labels on this product page. “Direct thermal” is a technology description, not a guarantee that every generic roll or fanfold stack will feed and detect correctly.

The [compatible media guide](/guides/choose-compatible-thermal-labels/) explains liner, sensing and stock-size checks. The [label preview](/tools/label-preview/) can illustrate sizing and scaling; it cannot establish that a physical label is approved for this printer.
